Who we are

About Latin America Representative

Headquartered in Criciúma, Santa Catarina — Brazil — we exist to remove the friction from cross-Pacific trade. For years we have worked alongside Chinese manufacturers and Latin American importers, translating not just language but business culture, compliance, and logistics.

Our mission is simple: build trustworthy commercial bridges that last. We are not a marketplace and we are not brokers chasing commissions — we are a representation firm that puts its name on every deal we facilitate.

  • Service in 3 languages: English, Portuguese, Spanish

  • On-the-ground support across Latin American markets
